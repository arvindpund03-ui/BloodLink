🩸 About BloodLink

BloodLink is a Blood Donation Management System developed using Django. This website helps patients find blood donors quickly and allows donors to register and help save lives.

Features

  • Donor Registration
  • Blood Request Management
  • Search Donors by Blood Group and City
  • Matching Donors
  • Dashboard Statistics
