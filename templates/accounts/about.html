About BloodLink

BloodLink is a digital blood donation and emergency support platform designed to connect patients, donors and blood requirements in a simple, fast and meaningful way.

❤️

What is BloodLink?

BloodLink is built with one important goal — making blood donation and blood requests easier to manage. The platform brings donors and patients together through a simple digital system.


Donors can maintain their profile, blood group, availability and donation information, while patients can submit blood requests and emergency requirements when support is needed.

🎯

Our Mission

To create a reliable digital bridge between people who need blood and people who are willing to donate.

🩸 Every donor can become someone's hope.
